Learning paths

Beginner: read the sequence

Vanadium is the trigger, potassium and iodine form an alternating-field analogy, arsenic and lithium form the directional/mobile backbone, and boron is the proposed structural memory layer.

Engineering: build a safe phantom test

Use a harmless dielectric grid with known holes, a low-voltage capacitance probe, an XYZ stage, calibration data, and a comparison between ground truth and reconstructed cells.

Physics comparison

Compare every model prediction against accepted atomic, electrochemical, semiconductor, and materials-science explanations. A useful hypothesis must make measurable predictions that can fail.

Security: protect real keys

Use passkeys, a secure element, authenticated encryption, rate limits, signed audit records, recovery controls, and independent review. Do not rely on obscurity or a visual simulation.
