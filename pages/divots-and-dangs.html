Conceptual theory / visual interpretation

Watson Particle — Shadow-Shell Study

The two dark line forms are interpreted as the front and back faces of a Watson particle. Their shadowed sides suggest that the particle can exist at many scales: a tightly compressed inner-core energy may remain small while its hydrogen-facing shell expands outward like a shadow.

In this conceptual model, hydrogen can front or encompass the inner core, forming a surrounding electron-like shell whose reach may become larger than the concentrated proton-like center. Light may extend beyond that boundary, but the particle’s own shadow-shell is treated as a persistent containment structure that would require significant work to compress.

The new addition

A dang and a divot are not separate species. They are complementary surfaces of one relationship:

Dang ⇄ Divot

The dang projects. The divot receives. Either can flip, expand, compress, lock, mismatch, or trade places with the other.

Resonant seed level

The seed module uses the project idea of Resonant Precipitation:

Mineral field → divot cavities → dang instructions → lattice match → seed-pattern emergence

Established biology explains seeds through reproduction, genetics, cell division, metabolism, and environmental conditions. This level is a speculative geometry overlay, not a claim that cannabis seeds appear directly from minerals.

Feather-color level

Divots become optical cavities and dang spacing changes reflected color. This connects the game to real structural coloration while keeping pigment and structural effects separate.

Alpha–Beta–Gamma–Delta

Alpha is the starting shape. Beta is the active board. Gamma is the target. Delta is the allowed shift that moves a pair outside its original alignment.
