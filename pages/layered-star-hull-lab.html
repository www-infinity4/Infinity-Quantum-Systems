    REFERENCE & HYPOTHESIS
    How the inner core is represented

    Hydrogen, helium, and lithium form a conceptual source-buffer-transition stack. The lab turns that sequence into visual scores rather than physical reactor calculations.

    Why layers 4–6 matter

    Beryllium is shown as a reflector analogy, boron as the control layer, and carbon as the structural and thermal hull. Real material behavior depends on form, temperature, impurities, irradiation, interfaces, and manufacturing.

    How layers 7–9 are treated

    Nitrogen, oxygen, and fluorine are represented as field or plasma-boundary concepts. The site does not recommend storing reactive fluorine gas or constructing an energized plasma vessel.

    What Ytterbium contributes

    Ytterbium provides the precision-clock inspiration. Timeline interception and planet-saving loops remain project hypotheses and science-fiction world mechanics.

    Civilization inside a loop

    The scenario explorer treats an Infinity loop as continuous environmental renewal, archival memory, reversible decisions, and resource recirculation—not literal immortality.
